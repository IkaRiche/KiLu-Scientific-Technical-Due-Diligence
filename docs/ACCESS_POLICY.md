# Disclosure and controlled-review policy

This public repository is a product and research **orientation surface**, not a code distribution or technical design disclosure.

## Public information

Public-facing material is limited to the business problem, intended use cases, qualitative capabilities, the existence of scoped testing and explicit boundaries of the claims.

## Restricted technical evaluation

The rights holder may consider successive access levels only after confirming identity, the transaction purpose and the precise technology scope:

1. **Initial screening:** high-level capability overview and evaluation objectives. No private source.
2. **Confidential technical evidence review:** under an appropriately executed confidentiality and permitted-use agreement, selected results, measurement conditions, reproducibility summaries and limitations can be examined.
3. **Source-level diligence, if authorized:** narrowly scoped controlled inspection under separate written conditions covering permitted use, access, copies, retention, derivative work, evaluation personnel and return/deletion of materials.

**An NDA by itself does not create a right to access source code.** An invitation to review does not promise transfer of every experiment, repository, algorithm or invention.

## Proprietary materials not published here

- Non-public source, complete algorithms and internal physiological or execution mechanisms.
- Internal interfaces, parameter choices, traces, prompts, fixtures, proof objects and raw experiments.
- Security-sensitive configuration, keys, unpublished patent ideas, transaction details and customer information.

## Current status

No commercial terms, IP assignment, availability guarantee, certification or access entitlement are created by this repository. See [legal terms](../LEGAL.md) and [public evidence scope](PUBLIC_REVIEW.md).
