# Public evidence and qualification overview

This page is a **high-level screening summary**. It is not a substitute for access to original experimental records.

## What is available for scoped review

| Research area | Evidence type retained privately | Supported public conclusion |
|:--|:--|:--|
| Independent authorization and execution control | Implementations, bounded software tests and controlled execution experiments | Individual authority and verification functions have been exercised in defined test settings |
| Embedded feasibility | Firmware builds and measured experiments on microcontroller-class hardware | Selected components can operate on constrained devices in their tested profiles |
| Bounded homeostatic and disturbance regulation | Historical simulations, MCU tests and later native host integration studies | Selected internal state and restrictive-control mechanisms exhibit repeatable behaviour within their scoped experiments |
| Integration and recovery | Host integration tests, hardware experiments of separate components and negative controls | Some interoperability and separation properties are exercised; complete product-level qualification remains outstanding |

**The rows refer to different experiments.** They should not be read as an end-to-end proof of one deployed system or as measurements of a single integrated runtime.

## Boundaries of what is established

- Production security certification, sector-specific approval and universal protection are **not claimed**.
- Results for one component do not establish complete combined-device timing, energy use, memory requirements or endurance.
- Multi-source isolation, operation under previously unseen conditions and customer-level cost improvements require separate evaluation.
- An independently controlled execution path does not automatically protect execution routes that have not been integrated into it.
- Negative experimental findings and limitations are maintained in the private evidence record and are material to scoped diligence.

## Next step for a serious evaluator

Agree a specific use case, expected outputs, strong alternative baseline, acceptance metrics and confidentiality conditions. A controlled technical review can then examine the relevant original measurements and reproducibility materials **without indiscriminate transfer of the source portfolio**.
