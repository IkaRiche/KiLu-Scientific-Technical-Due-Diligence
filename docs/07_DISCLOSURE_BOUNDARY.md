# Public / restricted disclosure boundary

**Public summary only.** This is not a source distribution and does not constitute legal clearance for publication of new patent-sensitive information.

### Public
- High-level problem, roles and prospective integrations.
- Sanitised descriptions of already reported experiments and what each measured.
- Claim-level status, public-source links and explicit negative findings.
- Reproducible validation of this **documentation package only**.

### Restricted
- Private repositories and commits, branch names, closed-source code, architecture details sufficient for unauthorised reimplementation, production deployment configuration.
- Canonical unpublished proof objects, keys, seed material, raw logs, complete cryptographic vectors with sensitive coupling details.
- Unpublished inventions, patent claims/drawings, special-purpose algorithms and integration secrets.
- Customer records, usernames or tokens, live endpoints, session/device IDs, commercial valuations, buyer negotiations and transaction terms.

No private source, raw evidence or original proprietary proofs are offered to external readers, including automatically under NDA. Owner-operated demonstrations may be considered separately at the rights holder's discretion.

A public GitHub disclosure may affect patent/trade-secret rights; any newly proposed disclosure requires the rights holder's and qualified counsel's review. A restricted claim must remain externally `EVIDENCE_INSUFFICIENT` if the primary evidence cannot be inspected.
